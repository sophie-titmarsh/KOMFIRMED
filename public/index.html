    Lead-out

    Used to turn wind, air density and gradient into seconds. Lighter riders and aero positions feel wind more. A lead-out helps most on flat, fast segments and barely at all on steep climbs. Only ride close together where it's safe and legal.

    Strava is 4EVA but…

    …please be safe out there.

    Don't take risks that endanger yourselves or others. Make sure you follow the road rules, and aim to tackle segments when there's the least amount of traffic. Weekday rush hours are marked with a dot on the grid so you can steer clear of them.
